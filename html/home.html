Good to know

Questions, answered.

A few things to know before you start.

How do I find a potential blood donor?+

Open Find a blood donor, choose the required blood type, and use the available search options to explore potential donors.

Can I register as a donor?+

Yes. Use Become a donor to get started and share your willingness to help through the available registration flow.

Does a search result guarantee compatibility?+

No. Results are only a starting point. A healthcare professional must assess suitability and confirm compatibility using appropriate testing.

What should I do in a medical emergency?+

Contact local emergency services or the treating healthcare facility immediately. Do not rely on a website search for emergency medical care.
